Science, at its best, is a very itterative process, but most students aren't taught how to constructively critique others' work, nor how to process and act upon critiqes of their own work. Histories of critique being paired with penalties, such as deducted points, can also make students hesitant to share their work in preliminary stages. I compiled the following zine through a combination of my own experiences and a meeting of the Iler-CaraDonna lab group in which other lab members shared their advice. 

There is both a version suitable for printing and folding, and a plain-text version for screen-readers.
